ABOUT US

What is Shadow Anime?

Shadow Anime is an anime streaming and entertainment platform where users can watch anime content, explore anime libraries, view episode details, discover popular series, join community discussions, save watchlists, and experience anime in a premium futuristic interface.

The platform may include anime pages, movie pages, popular anime sections, watch pages, watchlist tools, community posts, trailers, anime metadata, thumbnails, posters, banners, and other anime-related features.

Platform Purpose

Our goal is to provide anime fans with a smooth, modern, and engaging anime platform featuring anime streaming access, anime information, trailers, community features, recommendations, watchlists, and premium anime browsing experience.

Streaming Policy

Shadow Anime may provide streaming access, embedded players, trailers, episode information, or external media sources. Streaming quality, availability, subtitles, dubbing, and language options may vary depending on the available source.

Content Policy

Shadow Anime may display anime titles, episode information, posters, banners, trailers, thumbnails, descriptions, metadata, and streaming-related content. Users must not upload illegal, abusive, harmful, copyrighted, or misleading material.

Age & Viewer Guidance

Some anime may contain action, fantasy violence, horror, emotional themes, mature storytelling, or intense scenes. Viewer discretion is advised, especially for younger viewers.

User Rules

  • Use Shadow Anime respectfully and legally.
  • Do not harass, abuse, threaten, or attack other users.
  • Do not post spam, fake links, scams, malware, or harmful content.
  • Do not attempt to hack, overload, exploit, or damage the website.
  • Do not misuse anime pages, watch pages, comments, or community features.

Community Rules

  • Keep discussions anime-related and respectful.
  • Mark spoilers clearly before posting story details.
  • No hate speech, bullying, personal attacks, or abusive language.
  • No illegal content, harmful links, or misleading promotions.
  • Respect different anime opinions, fandoms, and recommendations.

Privacy Policy

Shadow Anime may store watchlists, continue watching history, preferences, theme settings, liked posts, saved anime, search preferences, and community interactions using browser storage or connected platform systems. We do not intentionally sell private user data.

Local Storage

Some features may save data directly in your browser using localStorage. Clearing browser data may remove saved watchlists, continue watching data, community drafts, poll votes, and preferences.

External Links

Shadow Anime may contain links to external websites, video providers, trailers, or third-party services. We are not responsible for the content, security, accuracy, availability, or policies of external platforms.

Security Rules

Users must not attempt to break, bypass, exploit, scrape, spam, reverse engineer, attack, or overload Shadow Anime systems, APIs, servers, forms, community tools, or website features.

COPYRIGHT

Copyright & Intellectual Property

Anime copyrights, trademarks, logos, posters, characters, and media belong to their respective owners, studios, publishers, licensors, and creators. Shadow Anime does not claim ownership over third-party anime intellectual property.

If you are a rights owner and believe that any material connected to Shadow Anime violates your rights, you may contact the site owner/admin for review, correction, removal, or proper redirection.

DISCLAIMER

Platform Disclaimer

Shadow Anime is an independent anime platform. All anime titles, logos, characters, posters, trademarks, and media belong to their respective owners. Shadow Anime does not claim official partnership, ownership, or endorsement from anime studios, publishers, distributors, or trademark owners unless clearly stated.

Content availability, episode links, trailers, posters, anime details, streaming quality, subtitle options, and dubbing options may change over time.

ADS & PROMOTION

Advertisements & Promotions

Shadow Anime may display advertisements, promotional sections, affiliate-style links, banners, or sponsored placements in the future. Ads should not be used to spread scams, malware, misleading claims, or harmful content.

INFORMATION

Anime Information Accuracy

Shadow Anime tries to provide useful anime information such as titles, posters, episode counts, types, categories, descriptions, and release details. However, information may sometimes be incomplete, outdated, duplicated, or incorrect.

SAFETY

Abuse, Spam & Platform Protection

Users attempting to spam, exploit, abuse, attack, scrape, damage, overload, reverse engineer, or harm Shadow Anime systems, APIs, community areas, watch pages, forms, or services may be restricted or blocked from the platform.

UPDATES

Policy Updates

Shadow Anime may update this policy page when new features are added, website rules change, community tools are improved, streaming features are updated, or legal/safety requirements change.

Need Help?

If you discover broken episodes, incorrect anime information, policy violations, community abuse, harmful links, copyright concerns, or technical issues, please contact the Shadow Anime administration team.
