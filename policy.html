01

Privacy Policy

Shadow Anime does not ask users to create an account for basic browsing. Features like watchlist, continue watching, favorites, community posts, and poll votes may be saved in your browser using localStorage.

LocalStorage data stays on your device unless you clear browser data. We do not sell your personal data.

02

Terms of Use

By using Shadow Anime, you agree to use the website respectfully and legally. Do not spam, attack, abuse, scrape aggressively, or misuse any website feature.

Shadow Anime may update its layout, features, links, community tools, or policy at any time to improve user experience and safety.

03

Content & Streaming Notice

Shadow Anime is designed as an anime discovery, organization, and viewing interface. Anime names, images, descriptions, and links may come from available API sources.

If any content owner believes something should be removed or corrected, they can contact the site owner through the contact section.

04

Community Guidelines

  • Respect other anime fans.
  • Do not post hate, harassment, spam, or harmful content.
  • Mark spoilers clearly.
  • Keep posts related to anime, movies, manga, or the website.
  • Community posts may be stored locally in the browser.
