ABOUT US

What is Shadow Anime?

Shadow Anime is an anime discovery and entertainment hub where users can explore anime, movies, manga, trailers, watchlists, community posts, and anime updates in a clean futuristic UI.

Our Purpose

We help anime fans discover titles, view details, explore trailers, save watchlists, and find official/source links.

Content Policy

Shadow Anime may show anime metadata, posters, public details, trailers, and source links for discovery purposes.

User Rules

  • Respect all users.
  • No hate speech or harassment.
  • No spam, fake posts, or harmful links.
  • No attempts to hack, damage, or abuse the website.

Community Rules

  • Keep discussions anime-related.
  • Mark spoilers clearly.
  • Do not post abusive or illegal content.
  • Use respectful language.

Privacy Policy

Shadow Anime may use local storage for watchlists, continue watching, comments, polls, community posts, and preferences. We do not sell personal data.

Disclaimer

Shadow Anime is an independent fan-made platform. All anime names, posters, characters, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

IMPORTANT

Copyright & Source Links

Shadow Anime should be used for anime discovery, metadata, public information, trailers, watchlists, and community discussions. Any external links should follow the rules of their original platforms and content owners.

LOCAL STORAGE

How Shadow Anime Stores Data

Some features may save data inside your browser using localStorage, such as watchlist, continue watching, saved posts, poll votes, and user preferences. Clearing browser data may remove these saved items.

Need Help?

If you find broken links, wrong details, harmful posts, or policy issues, contact the Shadow Anime admin or report it through the community page.
